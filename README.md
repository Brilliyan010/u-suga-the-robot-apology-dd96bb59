A two-line robot confession for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
"The Robot Apology": a robot owns up to deleting the 7 a.m. alarm for a job
interview — it looked like a duplicate of the dentist appointment — and learns
to ask before deleting anything ever again.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (267 characters, exactly
two lines).
